1. Purchase Incentives
The state offers a purchase subsidy for electric two-wheelers, three-wheelers, and four-wheelers
registered within Karnataka. The baseline subsidy for electric two-wheelers is set at INR 20,000
per vehicle, subject to revision by the Department of Transport. Commercial electric three-wheelers
are eligible for an additional incentive of INR 10,000.

2. Road Tax and Registration
All battery electric vehicles (BEVs) registered in Karnataka are exempt from road tax and
registration fees for the first five years from the date of purchase.

3. Charging Infrastructure Targets
The state aims to install at least 1 public charging station per 3 km along major highways and
1 per 25 vehicles in urban wards by the end of the policy period. Charging station operators
are eligible for capital subsidies covering up to 25% of equipment cost, capped at INR 10 lakh
per station.

4. Manufacturing Incentives
EV and battery manufacturers investing in Karnataka are eligible for capital investment subsidies,
stamp duty exemption on land purchase, and electricity duty exemption for a period of 10 years,
subject to minimum investment and employment generation thresholds.

5. Policy Duration and Review
This policy is valid for five years from notification and is subject to a mid-term review after
two and a half years, during which subsidy amounts, charging infrastructure targets, and
manufacturing incentives may be revised based on adoption trends and fiscal capacity.

6. Environmental Goals
The policy targets a reduction in transport-sector CO2 emissions and aims for electric vehicles
to constitute a meaningful share of new vehicle registrations by the end of the policy period,
in line with the state's broader climate action commitments.
