1. Purchase Incentives
Maharashtra provides purchase subsidies for electric two-wheelers, three-wheelers, and
four-wheelers, with additional "early bird" incentives for buyers in the first phase of the
policy period. Subsidy amounts are higher for vehicles manufactured within the state.

2. Road Tax and Registration
Battery electric vehicles receive full road tax and registration fee exemption during the
incentive period, along with a scrappage incentive for replacing old fossil-fuel vehicles
with electric ones.

3. Charging Infrastructure
The state targets deployment of charging infrastructure along key expressways (e.g.
Mumbai-Pune) and mandates charging provisions in new residential and commercial building
codes above a certain size threshold.

4. Manufacturing and Employment
Capital subsidies, interest subsidies, and employment-linked incentives are offered to EV and
battery manufacturers, with higher incentive tiers for units located in designated industrial
clusters.

5. Policy Duration
The policy runs for five years from notification, with incentive slabs designed to taper down
over time to encourage early adoption.
