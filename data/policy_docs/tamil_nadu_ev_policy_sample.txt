1. Purchase Incentives
Tamil Nadu offers 100% road tax exemption and registration fee waiver on all battery electric
vehicles for the duration of the policy. Direct purchase subsidies are primarily targeted at
commercial EV fleets and public transport buses rather than private two-wheelers.

2. Manufacturing Focus
The policy places strong emphasis on attracting EV and battery cell manufacturing investment,
offering capital subsidy, SGST reimbursement, and stamp duty exemption for large-scale
manufacturing units, particularly in and around Chennai's automotive corridor.

3. Charging Infrastructure
The state encourages private participation in charging infrastructure through single-window
clearance and reduced electricity tariffs for charging station operators, without a fixed
per-kilometre station density target.

4. Policy Duration
The policy is in effect for five years from notification, with manufacturing incentives
structured as multi-year investment-linked packages.
